Our Story

Golden Fork Hotel & Restaurant started as a small family-run eatery over ten years ago. Today, we've grown into a full hotel and restaurant experience, but we've never lost the warmth and personal touch that our very first customers fell in love with.

Our Mission

To give every guest a memorable experience through great food, comfortable accommodation, and excellent, friendly service.
